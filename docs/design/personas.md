# Personas for journey simulation (docs/ux/journeys)

Hypothetical but grounded in the research (`docs/research/apps/README-marriage.md`, GeoPoll 2023: 36.7% of Ugandan app users seek a spouse/long-term partner;
GSMA: low mobile-internet use, entry-level phones expensive). They are test fixtures for the simulation harness, not real users.

| Persona | Who | Device / network | Mindset | What would make them leave |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Grace**, 26 | Nurse in Kampala, first dating app | Android 9, 2 GB RAM, intermittent 3G, ~200 MB data bundle | Curious, wary of scams | A form wall before seeing anyone; big downloads; confusing English |
| **Moses**, 34 | Engineer, Mbarara, serious about marriage | Mid Android, decent 4G | Wants verified women, will pay if convinced | Being asked to pay before seeing the value; fake-looking profiles |
| **Aisha**, 29 | Teacher, devout, privacy-conscious | Android 11 | Will not show her face widely; fears being seen by relatives | Photos visible to anyone without consent; unclear who sees her |
| **Brian**, 27 | Student, Gulu, tight budget | Android 8, prepaid data, mobile money only | Will try anything free, rarely pays | Hidden renewals; card requirement; paywall at first screen |
| **Sarah**, 41 | Returning (divorced), diaspora-adjacent in Entebbe | iPhone, good data | Tired of casual apps, wants seriousness | Casual-hookup vibes; no filters for intent |
| **Peter**, 52 | Widower, reads large text | Android, font scale 200%, TalkBack off | Low digital confidence | Small tap targets; tiny text; jargon |

Journeys to simulate (each with a screenshot at every step and a step/tap count): first launch → first profile seen; sign-up with a wrong OTP and recovery;
verification started from the first "Send interest"; verification fails once then succeeds; reaching the free-interest limit and seeing the membership offer;
declining the offer and continuing free; accepting the trial; receiving an interest while free; matching and chatting; offline mid-journey; 200% text.
