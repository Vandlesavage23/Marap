# ADR-0009: See first, verify to act, pay for reach

- Status: Accepted (product owner instruction, 2026-10-19)
- Context: v1 required phone OTP → consents → ID+selfie verification → an 8-step profile (≥2 photos, all core questions) → an explicit trial before a new user could browse (Bible §10.3, §10.7). The owner's hands-on test: bad UI, users are forced to commit before seeing the app. Research across 17 platforms (docs/research/SYNTHESIS.md) found none that asks for payment, a trial or ID verification before first value; verification gates actions or visibility, money is asked at high-intent moments.
- Decision: adopt docs/design/user-flow-v2.md: free sign-up and browsing (limited fields for unverified viewers), verification at the first act that affects another person, a short go-live, a useful free tier (receive/accept/chat, ⚙3 interests/week), membership selling reach and convenience, the 24 h no-card trial offered at the quota moment.
- Consequences: supersedes Bible §10.3/§10.7 and the monetisation sentence in CLAUDE.md §1 (updated). Safety is preserved server-side (verified-only contact and visibility, limited payloads, anti-scraping caps). Revenue timing shifts later in the journey; quota and trial lengths are config and must be tuned with funnel data. Evidence is search-based only — a hands-on competitor walkthrough and Ugandan user tests are required follow-ups.
- Rejected: keeping the verification wall (contradicts all evidence); fully open profiles to anonymous visitors (privacy/scraping); pay-to-read received interests (dark pattern, FTC precedent).
