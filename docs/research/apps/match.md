## 1. Snapshot

- **Positioning.** The original mainstream online-dating brand aimed at adults wanting a serious relationship; Match describes its typical user as in their 30s-50s and looking for a serious relationship (search summary of Match Group material). Founding year (often cited as 1995): not retrieved from a source in this session **[unverified]**.
- **Ownership.** Match Group (formerly IAC; Match Group incorporated Feb 2009 as a conglomerate including Match.com) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Match_Group). Match is part of the Evergreen & Emerging segment (later merged into "Everyone Everywhere" in the Q2 2026 10-Q) (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000891103/000089110326000130/mtch-20260630.htm). Match Group FY2025 revenue was flat at about $3.5B (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/891103/000089110326000025/mtch-20251231.htm). The FY2022 10-K cites Match as one of the brands whose payers decreased. Match-brand payers/revenue: not found.
- **Markets.** Many (US, UK .co.uk, Ireland etc. have localised sites). Uganda: not found.
- **Model.** Free registration; paid subscriptions for full communication and extras; add-ons (Boosts, Super Likes) buyable without subscribing (Google Play listing via search).

## 2. Sign-up / onboarding flow, step by step

**Source quality: weak for screens.** Reconstructed from review sites (https://www.datingadvice.com/online-dating/sign-up-for-match ; https://www.top10.com/dating-ca/reviews/match ; Google Play listing https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.match.android.matchmobile&hl=en_US).

1. **Account creation:** email address (or phone number per one source), date of birth and password, plus age and gender (review summaries).
2. **Profile form:** relationship status, who you are looking for, interests, appearance, lifestyle, values (top10). Google Play says you can **skip sections and come back later**, then start viewing profiles.
3. **Photo:** you can upload at once (which makes the profile visible to others) or **browse incognito** until you have a photo (datingadvice summary). Up to 26 photos allowed on a free account (one source).
4. **Values / personality tools:** Match has a "Core Values" tool listing 21 values such as Adventure, Ambition, Friendship (https://match.mediaroom.com/news-releases?item=94840 via search). Whether the values step or a personality test is part of required onboarding: **not found**.
5. **Browse** profiles, suggested matches including "Top Picks"/"Highlights".

**Permissions timing, error handling, time-to-first-value:** not found. By the Google Play statement the profile browse can start right after a short form, so time-to-first-profile is likely short (minutes) **[unverified]**.

**What the user sees before committing.** A free account lets you browse, search and send likes with no payment and no card (https://www.match.com/p/singles/start-dating-online-for-free-with-match/ , search summary). There is no card-on-file free trial in the free path; a separate 3-day paid-trial pathway is mentioned by some sources and reportedly needs a payment method **[sources conflict]**.

**Exact current free vs paid model (re-check live).**
- Match's official "Singles for Free" page (summary): free covers real conversations, detailed profiles, local search, safety tools, and **unlimited messaging with mutual matches**; free members can read incoming messages and reply to people they matched with; paid adds unlimited likes, read receipts, advanced filters, seeing who liked you.
- Third-party numbers vary: "up to 50 daily likes" (not confirmed on the page), "unlimited likes and winks", one free Intro a day plus daily Highlights (Forbes: https://www.forbes.com/health/dating/match-review/), "only Top Picks" messaging.
- Older (2020) pages say free users cannot read or reply to messages; the Match UK FAQ mentions a "Connect" subscription tier. Both look outdated.
- **Historical model:** free to register, paid to communicate. Current official wording moved toward free messaging with mutual matches. Marked **[unverified until checked in-app]**.

## 3. First-session experience and core loop

- **Introductions:** a searchable member directory plus algorithmic suggestions (Top Picks/Highlights); likes lead to mutual matches. A "Matched By Us" test curating one match per week from serious-intent questions (five-year plan, relocation) was reported in beta (https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/match-beta-tests-new-automatic-matching-algorithm/); current status unknown.
- **Pacing/limits:** daily likes cap on free (number unconfirmed).
- **Empty states and notifications:** not found. Notably, the FTC complaint below concerned notification and email adverts that said someone had messaged or liked a user.

## 5. Monetisation

- **Plans (US, third-party, vary):** Standard 1 month about $44.99-45.99; Premium 3 months about $34.99/month; 6 months about $22.99-25/month; 12 months about $18.99-20/month; multi-month plans billed upfront; auto-renew on; Private Mode about $9.99/month; Premium adds Boosts, read receipts, priority placement (https://www.swipestats.io/blog/match-com-review ; https://www.datingapps.com/cost/match/ ; https://www.datinggroup.in/match-com-cost-2026/). Reviewers disagree on whether cancellation removes access immediately. **US prices; none for Uganda.**
- **Paywall triggers:** reaching the daily like limit; seeing who liked you; unlimited messaging beyond matches; read receipts; filters.
- **Free trial:** none in the free path (above).
- **FTC and consumer criticism.**
  - 2019 FTC complaint: Match sent free users adverts telling them someone had messaged or was interested, many from accounts Match itself flagged as likely fraudulent, to push them to subscribe. Between June 2016 and May 2018, 499,691 subscriptions were bought within 24 hours of such an ad (allegation); Match's own document said cancellation "takes over 6 clicks" (https://fortune.com/2019/09/25/match-com-used-scammers-profiles-to-trick-its-dating-service-users-ftc-says ; https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/clarissajanlim/match-users-ftc-lawsuit-scammers).
  - Aug 2025 settlement: Match Group to pay **$14 million**, stop misrepresenting its six-month guarantee (hidden conditions such as keeping a primary photo approved within seven days of purchase), stop locking out users who dispute charges, and simplify cancellation; no admission of liability (https://www.ftc.gov/node/297440 ; https://ppc.land/match-group-settles-ftc-charges-for-14-million-over-deceptive-dating-practices/ ; https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/match-to-pay-14-million-overhaul-cancellation-and-guarantee-practices-in-ftc-settlement-081225.html). Charges: ROSCA and FTC Act s5(a).
  - 2026 ConsumerAffairs reviews report scam profiles several times a week (https://www.consumeraffairs.com/dating_services/match.html?page=3).

## 6. Safety and trust features

Safety tools are advertised as free (Match's free page). Verification: see section 4. The FTC matter is the main trust evidence, and the key lesson is that fraudulent-profile marketing damages trust more than any feature adds.

## 7. Accessibility and ease of use

No accessibility statement found. Usability: Trymata's comparison calls deleting a Match.com account "a nightmare, especially from mobile" (https://trymata.com/blog/ux-wars-okcupid-vs-match-com/). Ease at sign-up: quick, skip-able form (Google Play).

## 8. Why they do what they do, and transferable lessons

**Evidenced reasoning.**
- Free registration and browsing maximise the supply of profiles (needed for paying users to find value); payment is placed at communication, the point of highest intent.
- The FTC case shows what happens when conversion pressure outruns honesty: fake interest notifications and obscure guarantees.
- Move toward "free messaging with mutual matches" follows competition from free apps (inference; Match's page frames it as keeping essentials free).

**Lessons.**
1. **HIGH.** Registration, profile editing and browsing without payment (and without ID) is the industry norm; Match even allows browsing incognito before adding a photo. The product owner's complaint is supported by all five apps.
2. **HIGH.** Never imply interest or messages that do not exist to drive purchase (FTC). Notifications must reflect true events (our CLAUDE rule 9 aligns).
3. **HIGH.** Cancellation must be simple; settle on one-tap in-app and one web path, no retention maze.
4. **HIGH.** Keep payment off the path of a formed mutual match: free reply to a match removes the "pay to answer" resentment.
5. **MED.** Skippable profile sections with "finish later" lower first-session drop-off.
6. **MED.** Show clear limits (daily likes) rather than blur.
7. **MED.** Values/relationship-intent tools support serious-intent screening; relevant to marriage frame.
8. **LOW.** Guarantees (six months free) carry legal risk when conditional.
9. **LOW.** Price tiers by term ($19-46/month) do not transfer.

## 9. Anti-patterns to avoid

- Marketing emails or push saying "someone is interested" when the sender may be a bot or non-existent.
- Guarantees with hidden conditions.
- Locking users out of paid accounts when they dispute a charge.
- Multi-click cancellation (the FTC cited "over 6 clicks").
- Hard-to-find account deletion on mobile.
