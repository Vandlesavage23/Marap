# Africa-focused and local (Uganda, Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana) dating / matrimony apps

Access date for all URLs: 2026-10-19. Evidence caveat: **evidence here is thin.** Searches returned app-store listings, startup press and promotional pages; I could not open them directly and I found **no** Ugandan marriage-specific app with independent coverage. Statements marked **[unverified]** are self-reported or from marketing pages.

## 1. What I could substantiate

### Trueflutter (Nigeria) - best-documented
- Started as a WhatsApp matchmaking group in Jan 2021 by brothers Dare and Ayo Olatoye after speed-dating events; targets people "tired of casual dating apps" looking to marry; culture/values-aware matching; audio intros; optional private matchmaking after a virtual consultation (https://techpoint.africa/2023/03/16/nigerian-dating-app-trueflutter/ ; https://restofworld.org/2023/trueflutter-dating-app-nigeria/).
- 2023 stats (company-reported): 308k users, 2.1M matches, 44 marriages ("likely undercount"). Premium NGN 2,900/month (about USD 6.23): invisibility, location change, "Flutters" super-likes. Angel funding Dec 2020 (https://disruptafrica.com/2020/12/07/nigerian-dating-app-trueflutter-raises-funding-from-3-angel-networks/). Identity verification: none found.
- **Takeaway:** 44 marriages from 308k users (about 0.014%) illustrates how rare confirmed outcomes are; do not over-promise "marriage" outcomes in copy.

### Pacaya (Kenya)
Nairobi launch July 2022, "intentional dating"; preference-based matching (range, distance, interests, deal-breakers, optional ancestral home and religion), free Android (allAfrica: https://allafrica.com/stories/202207200151.html). The Product Hunt listing now describes a group-activity app, suggesting a pivot (https://www.producthunt.com/products/pacaya-app) - current status **[unverified]**.

### Ghana - Marrify (not a dating app, but highly relevant)
Launched Aug 28, 2026 by Marry Right Ghana Ltd with the Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection; lets people check whether a prospective partner is already married via Registrar-General, courts, assemblies, Gazette; covers ordinance, customary and Islamic marriages; also registers of gazetted marriage officers and imams (https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/app-verifying-pastors-imams-venues-for-marriages-launched/ ; https://yen.com.gh/ghana/311329-ghana-launches-marrify-app-people-verify-marital-status-prospective-partners/). Fees and mechanics unknown. **Takeaway:** an explicit marketplace demand for "is this person already married?" - a core Ugandan/East African marriage-seeker concern (inference).

## 2. Market evidence useful for Uganda
- GeoPoll survey, Nov 2023, Ghana/Kenya/Uganda, n=4,259 app users 18-60: among Ugandan respondents 36.7% are on dating apps seeking a spouse/long-term love; safety perception overall: 30.1% very safe, 33.7% somewhat, 26.8% neutral, 7.9% unsafe, 1.4% very dangerous (https://www.geopoll.com/blog/digital-dating-in-africa-geopoll-report/ , excerpt; per-country split of the safety figures not clear).
- Kaspersky (2020 coverage): fake dating-app malware attacks in Africa; most targeted South Africa, Kenya, Nigeria (https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/scammers-use-fake-versions-of-dating-apps-to-target-african-singles/). A Techpoint digest claim about Nigeria origin of fraudulent profiles is **[unverified]**.
- Statista: Uganda dating-app market "moderate growth"; Africa online-dating penetration 3.3% (2024) (https://www.statista.com/outlook/amo/app/dating/uganda/).

## 3. Observed patterns (inferred; tag as such)
1. Local apps compete on cultural fit and "serious" positioning, not on verification depth; ID verification is rarely documented.
2. Low ARPU (Trueflutter NGN 2,900/month) suggests Uganda price points must be small.
3. The few outcome numbers are tiny; trust is built through verification and safety, not marriage claims.

## 4. Lessons for Uganda
| Lesson | Uganda |
|---|---|
| Verified-identity marriage app is an open niche in East Africa (no documented competitor with ID+selfie in Uganda) | HIGH (inference from absence of evidence - treat cautiously) |
| "Is this person already married?" worry (Marrify demand) | HIGH: consider future marital-status attestation (Phase 5 stubs only) |
| Matchmaker/consultation human touch (Trueflutter) | MED |
| Low price tier, local currency, mobile money | HIGH |

## 5. Anti-patterns
Fake apps impersonating dating brands (Kaspersky); generic "Uganda dating" apps with no verification and keyword-stuffed titles; overselling marriage outcomes.
