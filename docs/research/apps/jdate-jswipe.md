Access date for all URLs: 2026-10-19. Evidence caveat: search excerpts only. **[unverified]** = not on an official page.

## 1. Snapshot and model
- JDate: long-running Jewish dating site/app. JSwipe: swipe app launched Passover 2014 by Smooch Labs, acquired by Spark Networks in Oct 2015 for USD 7M after patent/trademark dispute (https://www.datingnews.com/apps-and-sites/jswipe-a-jewish-dating-app-with-millions-of-users/ ; https://www.jta.org/2015/07/30/ny/david-yarus-vs-goliath). Whether JSwipe is still a separate live product today: **[unverified]**.

## 2. Sign-up / onboarding
Not documented step-by-step in sources found. [unverified]. Free vs paid per JDate's current help page: free users can message a match only on mutual like, view full photos and profile details of people they browse or like, send up to 10 likes/day, and see 3 daily Recommended Picks (https://support.jdate.com/hc/en-us/articles/21025016703644--Updates-to-Your-Jdate-Subscription-Benefits). The listed premium features (read receipts, see who liked you, anonymous browsing, etc.) come from a third-party review and may predate the update.
- Historical: older JDate blogs say non-paying users were notified of messages but could not read them or see the sender (2000s-era; **dated**).

## 8. Lessons for Uganda
| Lesson | Uganda |
|---|---|
| Show full photos/profile details to free users who engage (they can see what they like) | HIGH |
| Community-specific brand gives trust shorthand | MED (single "marriage-minded verified" brand rather than tribe/religion splits) |
| Swipe UI risks casual framing | LOW (we use description-based interest, not swipe) |

## 9. Anti-patterns
Letting free users see that someone wrote but not read it (historical; the same lure tactic criticised for Zoosk and in the FTC Match Group case).
