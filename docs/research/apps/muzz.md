# Muzz (formerly muzmatch)

Access date for all URLs: 2026-10-19. Evidence caveat: search excerpts only; Muzz help pages were seen via excerpts, not read in full. **[unverified]** = not on an official page.

## 1. Snapshot and model
- Muslim marriage app; swipe-style "like/match" but explicitly marriage-intent. Member claims range from 8M (2023, Rest of World) to 10M (2024) to 15M+ and "600,000 marriages" (Arab News 2025); company-reported, **[unverified]**: https://restofworld.org/2023/muzz-dating-app-muzmatch-growth-slows/ , https://www.arabnews.com/node/2626725/amp
- Markets: UK/US/Canada core; India (230k users, ~2,000 marriages per Rest of World 2023), Pakistan (740k users, 6,200 marriages per Gulf News via GDI, **[unverified]**), Indonesia, Turkey, GCC. No Africa/Nigeria evidence found. https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/how-muzz-reinvented-marriage-seeking-in-pakistan/
- Rest of World: growth slowed after 2021 and Muzz struggled to replicate global success in India; users and experts blamed weak UX and incomplete encryption (same URL).

## 2. Sign-up / onboarding
- Google Play: "completely free to Like, Match, and Chat when both members like each other and are within their limits" (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.muzmatch.muzmatchapp). Free tier has limits (exact numbers **[unverified]**).
- Profile asks branch of Islam, practice level, marriage/children timeframe (French press via search: https://www.seneweb.com/en/news/Societe/saint-valentin-pour-trouver-lame-soeur-a-chaque-religion-ses-applis_n_483387.html); intentions are highlighted in the store copy.
- **Selfie verification is compulsory** per Muzz's safety guidelines; ID verification is optional (https://muzz.com/us/en/safety/profile-quality-guidelines/ ; https://muzz.com/us/en/help/profile/can-i-use-muzz-without-id-verifying-my-profile/). At what screen the selfie is demanded and whether you can browse first: **[unverified]**.

## 3. Verification
- Selfie (live, forward-facing; old photos rejected; never shared) - https://muzz.com/us/en/help/profile/can-i-use-an-old-photo-for-id-or-selfie-verification/ ; fallback to another device if camera fails (https://muzz.com/us/en/help/getting-started/i-cant-take-a-selfie/).
- Optional ID check (driver licence/passport) -> blue tick; Muzz says it does not keep ID copies (older post, ~825 days old: https://muzz.com/us/en/blog/muzz/id-verification/).
- **Wali / chaperone**: a user can bring a trusted family member into conversations; Mozilla confirms the existence of an in-app chaperone option (https://www.mozillafoundation.org/es/privacynotincluded/muzz/); GDI describes a "Wali" option that lets users "invite a trusted family member into conversations" and lists it as a free feature (https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/muzz-officially-launches-in-indias-muslim-marriage-market/). Whether the wali is a live participant or a transcript recipient: **sources differ** (ProPakistani: transcripts sent to wali, https://propakistani.pk/2024/05/17/muzz-is-putting-rishta-aunties-out-of-business/). Exact mechanism **[unverified]**.
- Photo privacy: members "keep photos private until ready to share" (App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/muzz-where-muslims-marry/id969997496).

## 4. Discovery
Swipe/like, filters (location, interests, profession, ethnicity, language, education; Gold adds advanced filters); mutual like -> chat. Gold lets you see whether you meet another member's filters before liking, and has a 10-slot active chat limit (https://muzz.com/us/en/help/gold/what-is-muzz-gold/ , excerpt). A user report of being locked out of browsing with five open conversations is a single Play-store anecdote, **[unverified]**.

## 5. Monetisation
Gold weekly/monthly/quarterly/biannual/annual (Google Play); 2 free boosts/week, undo, invisible mode, enhanced filters, daily compliments. Prices conflict wildly: USD 14.99/mo (2026 review), 19.99-39.99 (older), GBP 19.99 (UK review) - **[unverified]**. Trustpilot complaints: fake profiles/bots, blocked accounts without explanation, charged after trying to delete profile, Gold + boost yielding little visibility (individual, unverified): https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/muzz.com

## 6. Trust & safety UX
Compulsory selfie, moderation, blue-tick ID, wali option, controlled visibility. Muzz also markets to Pakistani families and runs mother-and-singles events (Dawn: https://aurora.dawn.com/news/1145496), and ran a campaign acknowledging families' role while pushing couple compatibility (GDI: https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/muzz-releases-thoughtful-matchmaking-campaign/).

## 8. Lessons for Uganda
| Lesson | Uganda |
|---|---|
| Wali/guardian as an *opt-in, in-product* feature (free) rather than pretending families do not exist | HIGH (adapt to Christian/Muslim/customary contexts: "trusted family member/pastor/imam" - inference) |
| Free like-match-chat core; Gold sells convenience (boost, filters, undo) not access | HIGH |
| Compulsory live selfie, optional ID | MED-HIGH (our product mandates ID; Muzz shows optional ID works at scale) |
| Intent fields (timeframe, practice) as first-class | HIGH, subject to our rule that religion data is optional/consent-gated and never used in ranking |
| Events with mothers/families | LOW for v1 |

## 9. Anti-patterns
Weekly auto-renewing subscriptions with confusing price points; unexplained account blocks; chat-slot limits that feel punitive; perceived bots.
