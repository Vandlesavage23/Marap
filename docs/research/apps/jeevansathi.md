## 2. Sign-up / onboarding
- Official help centre: "registration is simple & free of cost ... not more than 5 minutes" (https://jeevansathi.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/5000703780-how-can-i-register-on-jeevansathi). Third-party review: email + mobile + password first, 10-15 minutes for the whole form (https://www.datingscout.com/jeevansathi/review).
- Official FAQ: "all members whether free or paid can create a profile, create album, define partner preference, search and express interest" (https://static.jeevansathi.com/P/faq_main.php?checksum=&tracepath=31). Messaging requires a paid plan.
- Contact details: FAQ says contact details are shown to all members regardless of plan *if they match your filter criteria*; review sites say contact numbers are premium. **Conflict - [unverified]**. Store listing lists phone/email viewing as a premium benefit (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jeevansathi-com-marriage-app/id969994186).
- A Kayastha directory page claims a 400-contact cap for free members: **[unverified]** (https://matrimony.notion.site/Best-Kayastha-Marriage-Bureau-b9a921c2e4404fca8f466fdc83364891).
