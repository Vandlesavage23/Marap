# BharatMatrimony (Matrimony.com Ltd)

Access date for all URLs: 2026-10-19. Evidence caveat: direct page fetches were blocked in our sandbox; facts come from search-result excerpts. **[unverified]** = could not read from an official source.

## 1. Snapshot and model
- Operated by Matrimony.com Ltd (also CommunityMatrimony, EliteMatrimony). Reported 2.72 lakh new paid subscribers in a recent quarter and matchmaking billing of INR 135.3 Cr (Q1 FY27, Inc42: https://inc42.com/buzz/matrimony-q1-profit-doubles-yoy-to-%E2%82%B919-cr-revenue-up-13/ ; figures from excerpt, **[unverified]** against filings).
- Model: free registration/search/interest; paid membership unlocks contact; a verified "Prime" tier; plus offline retail outlets (counts conflict: 120 vs 140, **[unverified]**).

## 2. Sign-up / onboarding
- Free: register, create profile, search, send interests, match suggestions/alerts, advanced filters (language, city, education, occupation) per store listing/Windows app page (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id465923141).
- Trustpilot reviewers complain that free registration lets you see nothing meaningful (messages, calls from interested members) until upgrade (https://dk.trustpilot.com/review/bharatmatrimony.com , excerpt; individual claims).
- Screen-by-screen flow and time-to-first-value: **[unverified]**.

## 3. Verification
- **Prime** membership: requires university degree, government-ID verification and profile picture (https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/bharatmatrimony-launches-prime-membership-for-premium-users/).
- Vendor Bureau case study: OCR of ID, data checked against official sources, liveness selfie matched to ID photo; verified users go to "prime buckets" (https://bureau.id/resources/blog/how-bharat-matrimony-is-onboarding-trustworthy-users). Vendor marketing; treat as claim.
- Fields: community, religion, language, education etc. are core; horoscope exists in the category (we exclude).

## 4. Discovery
Search filters first, suggestions/alerts second; "Express Interest" or "Personalised Message" as contact mechanics (store listing). Contact reveal is premium: coupon pages describe plans limited by number of viewable mobile numbers (e.g., 50 on Gold, 70 "verified numbers" on Classic; **[unverified]**, third-party). **SecureConnect**: members can receive calls without revealing their mobile number (store listing excerpt) - a privacy mechanism worth noting.

## 5. Monetisation
Tiers (third-party prices, indicative): Gold 3 mo ~INR 5,300; Prime Platinum 3 mo ~INR 9,500 (https://www.cashaly.com/store/bharat-matrimony-coupons). Perks listed: WhatsApp/voice/video calls, "featured in premium section". Refund complaints appear on consumer portals (Voxya; self-reported, unverified): https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/premium-membership-/235624

## 6. Trust & safety
Prime verification tier, SecureConnect masked calls. No published fake-profile rate found.

## 7. Low-data / language / offline
- **Vernacular lite app (2018)** in 8 languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati), Android build "under 1 MB", optimised for 2G (company claims): https://www.mediainfoline.com/brand/bharatmatrimony-launches-lite-app-8-regional-languages
- PWA launched 2017 because >80% of profile views were on mobile: https://www.mediainfoline.com/brand/bharatmatrimony-launches-progressive-web-app
- Offline retail outlets (assisted sign-up) - suggests assisted onboarding matters for non-digital-native users.

## 8. Lessons for Uganda
| Lesson | Uganda |
|---|---|
| Lite/PWA channel and vernacular UI for low-end phones and data | HIGH |
| Verified tier as an *opt-in upgrade* (Prime) rather than a gate to see the product | HIGH |
| Masked calling instead of raw number reveal | HIGH (use in-app call/WhatsApp-bridge; mobile numbers are sensitive) |
| Assisted/offline onboarding via agents | MED (agent-assisted sign-up/mobile-money kiosks; privacy cost) |
| Selling by count of contact reveals | MED (clear, but makes users pay per number; see Aisle) |

## 9. Anti-patterns
Free tier perceived as "nothing works until you pay" (reviews); no-refund/undelivered-premium complaints; community/caste-style filters need strict handling (we do not do caste; analogous for tribe/ethnicity - inference).
