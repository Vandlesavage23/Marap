# Zoosk

Access date for all URLs: 2026-10-19. Evidence caveat: search excerpts only; most sources are third-party review sites. **[unverified]** = not on an official page. Zoosk is general-purpose (not marriage-specific); included as a "behavioural matchmaking + hard paywall" counter-example.

## 1. Snapshot and model
General dating app owned by Spark Networks (acquired July 2019; later controlled by MGG - low-quality aggregator, **[unverified]**: https://subger.com/en/service/zoosk). Features "Carousel", "SmartPick" behavioural matching and Coins.

## 2. Sign-up / onboarding
Reported ~5 steps: choose method (Facebook/Apple/Google/email), gender and seeking, birthday, photo, a few lifestyle questions (religion, smoking, kids, body type, ethnicity); onboarding fills ~20% of the profile; only two gender options in one review (https://www.swipestats.io/blog/zoosk-review ; https://www.datingnews.com/dating-sites/zoosk/ ; 2022-era guides, **may be stale**). Free members can browse Carousel but **cannot message or read messages**; Coins do not unlock chat (https://bestcompany.com/businesses/zoosk ; https://vator.tv/?p=32659).

## 3. Verification
Photo verification exists in the category; Zoosk specifics not established in sources found. [unverified]

## 4. Discovery
Carousel (swipe-like), SmartPick (behaviour-based suggestions), search; subscriber-only messaging and "who viewed me". 

## 5. Monetisation
Subscriptions (1/3/6/12 months), prices conflict: 1 month USD 29.95-34.99; 12 months ~USD 10-11.67/mo (https://www.datingadvice.com/online-dating/zoosk-cost ; https://www.vidaselect.com/zoosk-login-page/). Coins for boosts/gifts, bundles 180/480/1800. Auto-renew unless cancelled. Complaint themes (reviewers 4-5 years old): free members receive messages they cannot read until they subscribe; "bogus messages" suspected; former activation fee (now dropped). Zoosk's reply on Trustpilot: "With our paid subscriptions, you can enjoy unlimited messaging."

## 6-7. Trust & safety; accessibility
Not substantiated in sources found. [unverified]

## 8. Lessons for Uganda
| Lesson | Uganda |
|---|---|
| Hard message paywall + teaser notifications is the archetype to avoid | HIGH (as avoidance) |
| Coins that do not unlock core function confuse | LOW |
| Behavioural matching needs data volume | LOW at launch |

## 9. Anti-patterns
Teaser messages from subscribers that non-subscribers cannot read; coin economy layered on a subscription; renewals that surprise.
