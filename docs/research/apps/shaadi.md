# Shaadi.com (and Sangam.com)

Access date for every URL below: 2026-10-19.

**Method and evidence caveat (read first).** Our sandbox could not open pages directly (WebFetch failed DNS; the egress proxy refused `support.shaadi.com`). All facts below come from web-search result excerpts of official help-centre pages, app-store listings, court reports and press. I did not walk the live sign-up flow. Where a step order, price or limit could not be read from an official source it is marked **[unverified]**. Prices are from coupon/third-party sites and are indicative only.

## 1. Snapshot and model
- India's best-known matrimony service; sells paid tiers (Gold 3 mo / Diamond 6 mo / Platinum 12 mo, each with a "Plus" variant) per Shaadi's own FAQ: https://www.shaadi.com/info/customer-relations/faq/membership
- Core model: free registration + search + shortlist; **paid membership unlocks initiating chat and viewing phone/email**. App-store text: "Premium members can view phone numbers and email IDs ... send personalised messages and start chats" (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shaadi-com-matchmaking-app/id480093204).
- Sister product **Sangam.com** targets *parents* searching for partners for their children, emphasising family and background details (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1398753156). This is the clearest evidence of a family-side product from a large incumbent.
- Academic framing: Shaadi.com sells "arranged love" (https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/cross_fac/iatl/research/reinvention/archive/bcur2013specialissue/tuxen/ , abstract only read).

## 2. Sign-up / onboarding
- Registration is free; free users can "register, search by community, city or profession, shortlist matches" (App Store listing above). Deccan Herald (third party) says applying for matches and chatting is also free (https://www.deccanherald.com/connect/5-best-matrimonial-sites-in-india-for-finding-your-perfect-punjabi-match-4131460); this **conflicts** with Shaadi's support article that premium members "start chats ... without a limit" (https://support.shaadi.com/support/solutions/articles/48000953223-what-advantage-do-i-have-in-chatting-as-a-premium-member-). Treat free chat initiation as **[unverified]**; the official sources point to premium-only initiation.
- Free vs paid: free = profile, browse, search filters, shortlist. Paid = initiate chat, view contact numbers/emails, personalised messages, profile boost.
- Step-by-step screens, number of fields, and time-to-first-value: **[unverified]** (not observable from sources). Third-party Android app size: the iOS listing reports ~358 MB and English-only UI (https://apps.apple.com/np/app/shaadi-com-matrimony-app/id480093204 , via search excerpt).
- A support article exists titled "I have been asked to complete verification, I don't want to do this and delete my profile" (https://support.shaadi.com/support/solutions/articles/48000755498-i-have-been-asked-to-complete-verification-i-don-t-want-to-do-this-and-delete-my-profile). Its existence implies verification is requested at some point and users resist it; whether it blocks browsing is **[unverified]**.

## 3. Verification
- **Selfie verification** -> blue tick; selfie matched to profile photo; a photo must exist first; selfie "used only for verification and not shown or shared"; photo can be set "Only visible to members I like" while verifying (https://support.shaadi.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007907-what-is-selfie-verification-and-why-is-it-needed- , .../155000007909- , .../155000007908-).
- **Phone verification as a consent handshake:** if a member's number is unverified, "View Contact No" turns into "Request Verification"; the member must accept before you see the number; requests appear under a "Requests" tab (https://support.shaadi.com/support/solutions/articles/48000953219-how-does-the-phone-verification-request-feature-work-to-get-contact-details-). One help article also says verification matters for viewing contact numbers (https://support.shaadi.com/support/solutions/articles/155000008335-why-am-i-not-able-to-view-the-contact-number-of-some-matches-); exact rule **[unverified]**.
- Truecaller partnership for caller identification/safety (https://www.itvoice.in/shaadi-com-and-truecaller-partner-to-elevate-customer-experience-and-user-safety).
- Shaadi's blog says it uses AI, human review and system checks (https://blog.shaadi.com/safeguarding-your-journey-how-shaadi-com-protects-against-matrimony-fraud/ , excerpt only); no fake-profile rate published.
- Family section on profile (family details, siblings): https://support.shaadi.com/support/solutions/articles/48001202981-how-do-i-update-the-family-details-on-my-profile- . Community/religion/horoscope fields exist on the product (App Store lists community search); we exclude horoscope. No official "parent-managed profile" feature found; treat absence as **unconfirmed**.

## 4. Discovery
Search filters + suggested matches + shortlist; "interest"/chat initiation gated by paywall; contact reveal gated by (a) paid plan and (b) the other member's verified-number consent. Paid chat feature lets a member share contact details in a message when the other is offline (chat support article above).

## 5. Monetisation
- Tiers: Gold/Diamond/Platinum (+Plus) (FAQ above). Prices (coupon sites, INR, all **[unverified]**, conflicting): Gold ~3,858; Diamond ~5,542-6,950; Diamond Plus ~6,969; Platinum Plus ~11,308-14,850 (https://www.grabon.in/shaadi-coupons/ and https://www.talkcharge.com/store/shaadi). App-store IAP priced in USD for non-India.
- **Regulatory/legal:** Madras High Court granted an interim injunction (reported Jan 2025) against Shaadi's "30-day money back guarantee" ad after rival Matrimony.com sued: refund required sending >=10 interests with zero acceptances in 30 days; court noted risk of misleading the public (https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/madras-high-court/madras-high-court-shaadi-misleading-advertisement-violates-rules-282430 ; https://www.storyboard18.com/advertising/madras-hc-halts-shaadi-coms-ad-over-30-day-money-back-guarantee-misleading-claims-55052.htm). Interim order only.
- Telangana High Court refused to quash an FIR naming Shaadi.com's director in a fake-profile fraud case; police alleged insufficient verification (allegation, not a finding): https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/telangana-hc-fir-against-shaadi-dot-com-director-in-fake-profile-fraud-case-enn25042502959
- Third-party reviews of Sangam report fake profiles, investment-scam attempts and refund difficulties (aggregator reviews; indicative only): https://spark.mwm.ai/kr/apps/sangamcom-matrimonial-app/1398753156

## 6. Trust & safety UX
Blue tick; photo privacy ("visible only to members I like"); phone-number consent handshake; report-profile route for wrong numbers (support article above). Safety is clearly a problem area (court actions above).

## 7. Accessibility / low-data / language
App-store listing: profiles available across 9 Indian languages but UI shown as English-only; heavy app (~358 MB). 2009 SMS/WAP service existed in 9 languages (https://www.medianama.com/2009/03/223-shaadi-do-consumers-care/). No lite app found (contrast: BharatMatrimony lite app, see `bharatmatrimony.md`).

## 8. Why they do it / lessons for Uganda
| Lesson | Rationale | Uganda |
|---|---|---|
| Free to browse/search/shortlist, pay to *initiate* and reveal contact | Matrimony value is a successful introduction; paywall is placed where intent is highest | HIGH (but see anti-patterns) |
| Contact reveal via mutual consent ("Request Verification") rather than pure payment | Reduces harassment of women, adds a step before phone numbers leak | HIGH |
| Selfie-matched blue tick with explicit "selfie not shown to others" copy | Trust badge without exposing private image | HIGH |
| Optional photo visibility ("only members I like") | Conservative users / women | HIGH |
| Family section on profile; separate parent-oriented app (Sangam) | Parents are decision-makers in many cultures | MED (design a lighter "family contact / guardian" concept) |
| Plan structure of 3/6/12 month commitments | Long sales cycle | LOW (Uganda users prefer small, short mobile-money payments - inference) |

## 9. Anti-patterns
- Conditional "guarantee" with onerous fine print (court-restrained).
- Fake-profile/fraud exposure from weak verification (FIR).
- Heavy English-only app in a low-bandwidth market.
- Multiple tier names with "Plus" variants confuse buyers (inference from FAQ structure).
