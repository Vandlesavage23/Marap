# Christian Mingle and ChristianCafe

Access date for all URLs: 2026-10-19. Evidence caveat: search excerpts only. **[unverified]** = not on an official page.

## 1. Snapshot and model
- **Christian Mingle** (Spark Networks, sister of JDate; Spark later controlled by MGG after a 2024 restructuring per a low-quality aggregator, **[unverified]**: https://subger.com/en/service/zoosk). Faith-based dating; "over 23 denominations" selectable (https://www.christianmingle.com/en/believe/christian-dating-app).
- **ChristianCafe** (Cupid Media family **[unverified]**): subscription site; **no permanent free tier**, 7-10 day free trial reported (https://www.christiancafe.com/guests/free-christian-dating ; https://www.top10.com/dating/reviews/christiancafe). No Africa-specific evidence found despite the query.

## 2. Sign-up / onboarding
- Christian Mingle: sign up with email, Facebook, Google or Apple; real first name, date of birth, gender, partner preference; must be 18+; may be asked to verify age/identity (https://support.christianmingle.com/hc/en-us/articles/51216112880788-How-Do-I-Register). Denomination/church attendance are profile and filter fields (could not confirm they are asked at sign-up).
- **Free (Basic)**: 10 likes/day, 3 recommended picks, and *messaging mutual matches is open to all*; premium adds unlimited likes, up to 10 picks, "See Who Liked You", premium preferences, read receipts. Messaging without matching first was *removed* for everyone (https://support.christianmingle.com/hc/en-us/articles/28224292446748--Updates-to-Your-ChristianMingle-Subscription-Benefits ; https://support.christianmingle.com/hc/en-us/articles/21664203573532-Compare-Our-Basic-and-Premium-Subscriptions). Third-party 2026 reviews still say messaging needs premium - outdated vs official pages.
- ChristianCafe free trial: complete profile, email, winks, IM, forums; **25 messages/day cap** for trial members (official FAQ via excerpt). Payment needed after trial.

## 3. Verification
Christian Mingle selfie check -> blue shield badge; start from login pop-up or My Profile; **can skip and verify later**; no-camera path via QR/email link; greyed badge = failed (https://support.christianmingle.com/hc/en-us/articles/51216984686740). Photos reviewed before appearing. No ID verification found. Denomination shown as a profile attribute.

## 4. Discovery
Likes/recommended picks + filters (denomination, worship habits, location). Mutual like -> message. "Messaging+" add-on described by ConsumerAffairs (message anyone) - third party, **[unverified]** (https://www.consumeraffairs.com/dating_services/christian-mingle.html).

## 5. Monetisation and regulators
- Prices conflict: up to ~USD 29.99/mo (one review) vs from 49.99 (ConsumerAffairs); ChristianCafe iOS IAP 39.99/1 mo to 109.99/12 mo (App Store excerpt): https://apps.apple.com/app/id369712367 - all **[unverified]**.
- **2018 California settlement** (Santa Monica City Attorney-led task force): Spark Networks (JDate, Christian Mingle) paid USD 500k penalties plus up to USD 985k restitution for auto-renewals without express consent; must disclose renewal terms, use a separate consent checkbox, send post-payment summary, allow easy cancellation: https://santamonica.gov/press/2018/10/11/dating-sites-christian-mingle-jdate-settle-consumer-protection-case-with-california-prosecutors
- **FTC v. Match Group, Aug 2025, USD 14M** (different company but same category): deceptive guarantee conditions, hard cancellation, fake-message notifications luring non-subscribers (~500k subscriptions within 24h of such ads, 2016-18): https://www.insideprivacy.com/consumer-protection/ftc-secures-14-million-settlement-with-match-group-over-deceptive-subscription-practices/

Selfie badge, photo review, skippable. Weak vs marriage platforms: no ID, no family hook.

## 8. Lessons for Uganda
| Lesson | Uganda |
|---|---|
| Free tier with real daily allowance (10 likes + mutual-match chat) | HIGH |
| Faith as a first-class, *optional* attribute | HIGH (Uganda is 82% Christian per census; keep optional/consent-gated per CLAUDE.md rule 4) |
| Skippable selfie ("verify later") | MED - our policy requires verification before interaction; allow *seeing* before verifying |
| Separate consent checkbox for renewal | HIGH (also aligns with our no-dark-patterns rule) |

## 9. Anti-patterns
Auto-renewal without clear consent (settled); lure messages to non-subscribers (Match Group FTC); trial caps (25/day) that make a trial feel crippled; confusing messaging rules that changed (third-party pages now wrong).
