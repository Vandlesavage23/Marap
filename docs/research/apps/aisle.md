# Aisle (Aisle Network, India; Info Edge/Jeevansathi-owned)

Access date for all URLs: 2026-10-19. Evidence caveat: search excerpts only; many sources are third-party review blogs with conflicting numbers. **[unverified]** where not on an official page.

## 1. Snapshot and model
- Curated, relationship-minded app for urban Indians; sibling apps Anbe, Arike, Neetho, Jalebi (regional-language/community dating). Info Edge subsidiary Jeevansathi held 89.17% by March 2025; FY25 turnover ~INR 39.6 Cr (net result sign conflicts across sources): https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/info-edge-invests-1-1m-to-stabilize-aisle-network/ , https://www.angelone.in/news/stocks-share-market/info-edge-to-increase-stake-in-aisle-network-with-rs-30-crore-investment
- Early positioning: "balances traditional marriage values and modern dating" (YourStory 2015: https://yourstory.com/2015/08/app-fridays-aisle).

## 2. Sign-up / onboarding
- Mobile-number sign-up, basic info and photo verification (DatingScout: https://www.datingscout.co.uk/aisle/review). Older reports describe a team check before listing ("verifies credentials ... if user fits criteria, added to the community") - **dated, [unverified]** for today.
- Browsing profiles free; receiving invites free; **sending** invites limited/paid (third-party; conflicting numbers). Time-to-first-value **[unverified]**.
- Paywall design is catalogued at Adapty (could not read it; only referenced): https://adapty.io/paywall-library/aisle-indian-dating-app/

## 3. Verification
Photo/selfie verification is required for some actions; in a 2023-24 era campaign women got free premium features *only after completing verification* (https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/aisle-app-offers-free-premium-features-to-women/ , via excerpt). Verification as a reward gate. Religion/community filters exist; horoscope not relevant.

## 4. Discovery
**Invite model, no swiping.** You browse and send an invite; chat opens on acceptance; a rejected invite is returned to the sender, encouraging deliberate use (ichhori guide: https://www.ichhori.com/2022/05/how-does-aisle-app-work.html - blog, **[unverified]**). "Concierge" tier for high-intent seekers (App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1001481078 , excerpt).

## 5. Monetisation
- Tiers named in the store: Plus (unlimited likes), Premium, Concierge. Weekly plans from INR 99 introduced around the 11th anniversary (Global Dating Insights: https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/aisle-network-marks-11th-anniversary-with-changes-to-premium-model/ , excerpt). Third-party price lists: Premium ~USD 14.99/mo, Concierge ~USD 31.99/mo (paywall tracker, **[unverified]**); invite packs in a review: ~USD 5.99 for 3, 29.99 for 25 (**[unverified]**); a 2015-era article said INR 2,000 for 3 invites.
- Review aggregation shows negative sentiment about fake profiles and "premium not leading to matches" (**[unverified]**, aggregated summary).

## 6. Trust & safety
Verification before engagement (per DatingScout), women-first incentives, human curation historically. Scammer complaints persist in reviews.

## 7. Accessibility / language
Regional sibling apps (Anbe - Tamil, Arike - Malayali, Neetho - Telugu, per Info Edge list). Pattern: community/language-specific apps rather than one global product.

## 8. Lessons for Uganda
| Lesson | Uganda |
|---|---|
| Scarce, intentional "interest" (invite returned if declined) reduces spam | HIGH (fits "interest delivers description immediately") |
| Free to receive, paid to send more -> receivers never pay to see who's interested | MED (careful: could look like extortion; see anti-patterns) |
| Weekly low-priced plans | HIGH for mobile money (inference: small amounts) |
| Women get verified-only free perks | MED (supports safety, but may skew ratios) |
| Per-invite pricing | LOW (feels like paying per rejection) |

## 9. Anti-patterns
Selling invites in packs at high prices; ambiguous "premium" outcomes; price confusion across weekly/monthly/invite-pack constructs.
