## 2. Sign-up / onboarding flow, step by step

**Source quality: weak.** No Pageflows/Mobbin teardown was found for CMB. The steps below combine several review sites (https://www.swipestats.io/blog/coffee-meets-bagel-review ; https://www.datingscout.com/coffee-meets-bagel/review ; https://www.zoosk.com/date-mix/online-dating/how-does-coffee-meets-bagel-work/ ; https://www.top10.com/dating/reviews/coffee-meets-bagel). Treat as reconstruction.

1. **Account creation.** Options reported: phone number, Apple ID, and Facebook (older versions required Facebook; sources disagree on whether it is still required: one says "requires linking Facebook", another says phone or Apple now works).
2. **Verification.** Phone path sends a code by SMS (one guide says four digits); DatingScout lists email verification as required.
3. **Basics.** Gender, preferred gender, date of birth, height, relationship goals, education, job, location (several guides). Some also mention full name and email.
4. **Preference questions.** "Up to five" lifestyle, value and personality preference questions, plus optional prompts (one review).
5. **Photos.** The app recommends at least three; DatingScout says a profile picture is optional at sign-up. Unreconciled.
6. **Done**, then wait for the first batch. Reviews put total time at about 5-10 minutes.

**Permissions and timing.** Not found (no source describes notification/location prompt placement).

**Skippable.** Photo may be optional per one source; prompts optional.

**Time-to-first-value.** The product's core promise is a daily batch around **noon**, so the first profile may arrive up to a day after sign-up; sources say an incomplete profile may delay the first matches (Swipestats summary). First Bagel timing for a brand-new account is **[unverified]**.

**Before committing.** Nothing browsable beforehand, as far as sources show. Sign-up and the daily bagels are free; no payment is asked.

**On failure.** Not documented.

## 3. First-session experience and core loop

- **Daily Bagel.** One curated match per day (originally); you Like or Pass within a time limit; mutual like opens chat (https://www.BestCompany.com/online-dating/company/coffee-meets-bagel , secondary). Time limit lengths (24h bagel expiry, 7-day chat expiry): not confirmed **[unverified]**.
- **Beans.** Earned by daily login, referrals, social follows, or bought; spent on features such as un-sending a like and "re-bagel" (secondary).
- **Discover / suggested.** Later additions for more volume: men reportedly get up to 21 "suggested Bagels" a day; Premium users get 8 free "Flowers" per month, more cost Beans (https://beyondages.com/coffee-meets-bagel-cost/ ; Swipestats). A daily cap on Discover: not found.
- **Preferences warning.** CMB's own product blog (Oct 2023) argues that being too specific in preferences may *lower* the quality of Bagels received (https://www.coffeemeetsbagel.com/blog/the-science-behind-setting-your-preferences-liking-bagels, opening only).
- **Empty states.** Users report a small daily pool and that in many cities there are too few members (https://blog.photofeeler.com/coffee-meets-bagel-review/ ; https://roast.dating/blog/no-matches-on-coffee-meets-bagel). That is the cost of curation at low density, highly relevant to a new market.
- **Notifications.** Not found.

## 4. Profile building

- **Minimum.** Basics, preferences; photos recommended (>=3); questions optional. Exact minimum: **[unverified]**.
- **Verification.** Optional "Verify my profile" at the top of profile; record a short **video selfie** matched to the main photo; verified badge on approval; result stored with a partner so it can be reused when new photos are added; deleting the account deletes verification data (official blog: https://coffeemeetsbagel.com/blog/real-connections-real-people-profile-verification-now-on-coffee-meets-bagel). Later reporting says every photo is checked, the vendor is Persona, and availability widened from US/CA to global (https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/coffee-meets-bagel-expands-verification-to-tackle-catfishing/). Review time: "minutes" (official) vs "typically within 24 hours" (another report).
- **What it unlocks.** The badge, plus company-claimed higher connection rates (see below). Whether non-verified users are limited: not found.
- **Adoption evidence (company-reported).** Within one month of the 2026 rollout 70% of active users verified, and 81% within three months; CMB says verified profiles connect more, measured by phone-number exchange, with no baseline given (https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/coffee-meets-bagels-selfie-verification-sees-matching-success ; https://www.therakyatpost.com/living/2026/08/05/before-you-fall-in-love-cmb-wants-to-make-sure-theyre-real/). GDI itself cautions the figure may reflect pressure to verify. Conflicting rollout dates (earlier official post vs "April 2026" in the summary) mean the timeline is **[unverified]**.

## 5. Monetisation

- **Free.** Profile, daily Bagels, messaging with matches (secondary).
- **Premium (US, third-party, vary):** about $34.99-35/month; 3 months about $74.99; 6 months about $119.99; 12 months about $179.99 (not all lists); a one-week option at $16.99; a "Mini" tier at $14.99/month. Beans: 100 for $1.99, 2,000 for $23.99, 3,000 for $24.99 (https://beyondages.com/coffee-meets-bagel-cost/ ; https://www.swipestats.io/blog/coffee-meets-bagel-review). **US prices; Uganda pricing not found.**
- **Paywall triggers.** Seeing who liked you; extra likes/suggestions; read receipts; activity reports; Beans needed to extend an expiring chat and for flowers beyond the monthly quota (reviewer quotes via https://www.datingnews.com/apps-and-sites/coffee-meets-bagel-review/ and https://blog.photofeeler.com/coffee-meets-bagel-review/).
- **Trials / renewal.** Not found.
- **Criticism.** Trustpilot 1.4 stars from 59 reviews in a 2024 write-up (glitches, fake profiles, scammers asking for money) (https://new.tawkify.com/blog/dating-methods/coffee-meets-bagel-dating-app-reviews); Google Play reviewer: "the only way to match is to pay". Paywalling "who liked you" is a stated annoyance. No regulator action found. BuzzFeed News reported the algorithm showed users who chose "no ethnicity preference" mostly same-ethnicity profiles (https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/katienotopoulos/coffee-meets-bagel-racial-preferences) (older story).

## 6. Safety and trust features

Video-selfie verification, AI-generated-image screening, fraud detection, message moderation, reputation management and human review (CMB blog "inside CMB's approach to safe authentic dating": https://www.coffeemeetsbagel.com/blog/inside-cmb’s-approach-to-safe-authentic-dating). UX: a badge on profile and a "Verify my profile" prompt at top of profile.

## 7. Accessibility and ease of use

No accessibility audit or statement found. Ease: simple one-card-a-day model reduces choice overload (CMB positioning); weakness is a thin supply in smaller cities.

## 8. Why they do what they do, and transferable lessons

**Evidenced reasoning.** CMB's co-founder said women are more selective and prefer quality over volume while men prefer volume (secondhand Mashable quote on a low-quality site; verify before citing). The company calls its model friction by design. Beans and Flowers monetise the scarcity it creates. Verification followed catfishing and fraud pressure.

**Lessons.**
1. **HIGH.** Optional-but-visible video-selfie verification reached 70-81% adoption quickly in a market where the product also showed the badge; evidence (company) that verification need not be a wall to achieve high coverage.
2. **HIGH.** Reusing a stored verification result when photos change reduces repeat friction.
3. **HIGH.** Curation fails at low density. In a launch market with few members, a "one a day" feed will run dry; plan supply rules (broaden radius or show "no new introductions today" honestly).
4. **MED.** Telling users that over-narrow preferences lowers match quality is a useful honesty pattern for preference screens.
5. **MED.** A fixed daily batch time creates a ritual and a clear empty state ("next introductions at noon").
6. **MED.** Selling convenience (undo, re-introduce) rather than access is less hostile than gating basic matching.
7. **LOW.** Beans as a second currency adds complexity and drew complaints; not suited to mobile-money top-ups.
8. **LOW.** Facebook-linked sign-up is not relevant (phone-OTP is our base).

## 9. Anti-patterns to avoid

- Chat that expires unless the user spends currency (reviewer complaint).
- Paywalling the "who liked you" list with a teaser as the main upsell.
- "No preference" settings that silently behave like a preference (BuzzFeed finding).
- Undisclosed small pools presented as a curated "quality" feature.
