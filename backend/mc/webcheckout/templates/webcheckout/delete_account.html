Delete your Marriage Connect account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Settings → Privacy and account → Delete my account.
  2. Confirm with the 6-digit code we text to your phone.

What happens next

You have {{ grace_days }} days to change your mind by contacting us. After that your profile, photos, answers, messages and sign-in details are permanently erased.

We keep only what the law or safety requires: payment records for tax, a one-way fingerprint of your verified ID so a banned person cannot return, and records of safety reports.
